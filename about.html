What's the point of this?

Good question. Deep down, it's probably a vanity project to alleviate some guilt about not being home to do more to help after the recent devastating fires in Southern California. (just kidding, sorta)

Who am I?

I'm Parker. While I live in Detroit now, I grew up in Simi Valley and wanted to help in some way after the recent fires.

What does this site do?

This is a simple resource with two goals. The first, make it easy for anyone in SoCal to find a place they can volunteer and help out.

The second is to start a conversation with charity leaders and community organizers. I know this isn't solving the most pressing issues, but it's something I could build in a few hours from where I am now. I hope this is a conversation starter—that organizations on the ground see this and reach out with the more important technological needs they have.

Let's talk! If you're running an organization or are a community organizer and have more significant tech needs, please email me at HelpOutSoCal@gmail.com.
